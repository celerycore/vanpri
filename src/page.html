Keep it flowing. On hit, cancel light → medium → heavy → special → Liberty Art. Release and press each attack again for the next move.
Watch your meters. Land hits to charge Liberty. Assist recharges over time. Blocking drains your Calcium gauge; an empty gauge leaves you vulnerable. Crouch-guard against low attacks.
Play your way. Gamepad: left stick / D-pad to move; A / B / X attack, Y special, LB guard, RB assist, RT Liberty. Player two can use a second gamepad. On a phone, use the on-screen controls; landscape gives you more room.

About this edition.
This playable browser adaptation was built by studying the supplied Windows release, its 2D Fighter Maker 2002 data files, and game manual.
The ten main fighters, four partners, six stage backgrounds, music, and animation frames come from those supplied files. Combat, CPU behavior, balancing, menus, and one-button specials are newly implemented for the browser.
It is not an emulation or an exact port of the original engine. Original story sequences, the boss, alternate character variants, rollback networking, and the full original move system are not included. Local versus is shared-device play.
